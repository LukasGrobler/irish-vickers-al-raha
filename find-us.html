First time here?

  1. Head for Al Raha Beach Hotel

    We're on Rashid Bin Jabr Al Halami Street in Al Raha, right next to the hotel. Search “The Irish Vicker's Al Raha” in Google Maps.

  2. Park up

    There's parking outside and around the hotel. Staying there? It's a 30-second walk.

  3. Find the harp

    The building glows green at night, with the harp sign over the door. You can't miss it.

  4. Pick your side

    Inside for the stage, the bar and the pool table, or out to the beer garden on the grass.
